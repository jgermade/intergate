# Private subdomains shared with chosen people

Status: completed on branch `claude/dreamy-bell-6r8tt7`.

## The ask

"Para cada subdominio, añadiría un nuevo acceso, en private, añadiría otros
usuarios elegidos": for each subdomain, a private one can also be opened by
other users its owner chooses. It was also on the README's roadmap
("Sharing private subdomains with specific users").

Read as: *private* becomes "the owner and the people they choose", not a
fourth access level. With nobody chosen it is what private was before.

## What was built

- **Store** (migration 9, `internal/store/guests.go`): `subdomain_guests`
  (`id`, `subdomain_id` → cascade, `email`, `created_at`,
  `UNIQUE (subdomain_id, email)`). By email, normalized, so someone can be
  added before they have an account. `Subdomain.Guests` counts them.
  A rename keeps them (same id); deleting the subdomain removes them.
- **Rules** (`internal/manage/guests.go`): `AddGuest` validates the email,
  refuses the owner's own and duplicates, at most `GuestsPerSubdomain` (50);
  `RemoveGuest`. Both logged with the actor.
- **Proxy** (`internal/web/proxy.go`): `canAccess` became `mayOpen`; for
  private, the owner or `IsGuest(subdomain id, email)`, looked up on each
  request (one indexed query, next to the session lookup already there), so
  adding or removing someone works at once with the cookie they have.
  Whoever may not open it still sees the offline page.
- **Adding an email never lets anyone sign in**: the configuration and
  administrators still decide that. The dashboard says so rather than
  telling the owner whether an address may sign in, which the sign-in form
  is careful not to reveal either.
- **API**: dashboard `POST /api/subdomains/{name}/guests {email}`,
  `DELETE /api/subdomains/{name}/guests/{id}`; the detail has `guest_list`
  and `guests_limit`, every subdomain view `guests`. Admin socket: the same
  under `/v1/subdomains/{name}/guests`, `Client.AddGuest`/`RemoveGuest`.
- **Dashboard**: `components/access-guests.html` under the access choice
  while it is private ("Who else can open it": a tiles table like the SSH
  keys, a count badge, an email field with Add). Switching to public or
  signed-in users hides it and keeps the list for when it is private again.
  The private card's hint: "Only the owner and people they choose".
- **`intergate config`**: the subdomain shows who else can open it, with
  "Let someone else open it" and "Stop someone from opening it".

## Verified

`go test -race ./...` (new: `store.TestGuests`, `e2e.TestPrivateGuests`:
owner, guest, non-guest, removal on the next request, other users can't
manage them, admin from dashboard and socket). In Chromium against a local
server: add, duplicate message, removal, public/private switch, typing
surviving the page's 5-second reload, 390 px wide without horizontal scroll.
