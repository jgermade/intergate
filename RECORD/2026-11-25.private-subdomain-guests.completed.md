# Private subdomains shared with invited users

Status: completed on branch `claude/dreamy-bell-6r8tt7`.

## The ask

1. "Para cada subdominio, añadiría un nuevo acceso, en private, añadiría
   otros usuarios elegidos": a private subdomain can also be opened by
   other users its owner chooses. It was on the README's roadmap
   ("Sharing private subdomains with specific users").
2. "Usaría un typeahead que también permita seleccionar usuarios
   existentes": built, then replaced by 3.
3. "En las opciones de cada usuario, pondría una opción, poder invitar a
   otros usuarios, sólo si ya existen. Cambiaría el typeahead por un select
   con `<selectedcontent>`."

Read as: *private* becomes "the owner and the users they invite", not a
fourth access level. With nobody invited it is what private was before.

## What was built

- **Store** (migration 9, `internal/store/guests.go`): `subdomain_guests`
  (`subdomain_id` and `user_id`, both cascading, `UNIQUE` together) and
  `users.can_invite` (default 0). Guests are accounts, not emails: deleting
  an account removes it from what it was invited to. `Subdomain.Guests`
  counts them; a rename keeps them. Migration 9 was rewritten before any
  release (first by email; then by account, with `can_invite`).
- **Who may invite** (`manage.MayInvite`): users whose `can_invite` an
  administrator set, and admins and owners always (role from
  `cfg.UserRole`, so `auth.admins` counts). Set in **Admin → Users →**
  the sliders button, now "Options of …" (was "Limits of …"): a switch
  above the limits for users, a sentence for admins and owners. The table
  says "can invite others" under the user's subdomains. Admin socket:
  `PUT /v1/users/{id}/can-invite`; `intergate config` → a user → "Let
  them / Stop them inviting other users…".
- **Inviting** (`manage.AddGuest`, by account id or email): the owner must
  may invite; the account must exist, be enabled and may sign in; not the
  owner; once; at most 50. Inviting never lets anyone sign in.
- **Taking the right away keeps the list but closes the door**: the proxy
  (`web.mayOpen`) lets a guest in only while the owner may invite, looked
  up on each request with the guest check, so it applies at once and
  comes back if allowed again. The subdomain page then shows a warning over
  the list (people can still be removed); the wizard says so too.
- **Dashboard** (`components/access-guests.html`, under the access choice
  while private): the invited users (avatar or initial, name, email, when),
  and the theme's `Select` (`appearance: base-select`, `<selectedcontent>`)
  of the others with an Invite button, from
  `GET /api/subdomains/{name}/guests/candidates`: enabled accounts allowed
  to sign in, not the owner nor invited, sorted in the browser with
  `localeCompare` (SQLite's `lower` is ASCII-only: María came after
  Marta). Without the right: "Only you can open it. To invite other users,
  an administrator has to allow it in your options."
- **API**: `POST /api/subdomains/{name}/guests {user_id | email}`,
  `DELETE …/guests/{id}`; the detail has `guest_list` (with `user_id`,
  `name`, `avatar_url`), `guests_limit` and `can_invite` (the owner's);
  `PATCH /api/admin/users/{id} {can_invite}`. Admin socket: the same guest
  routes under `/v1`, `Client.AddGuest(name, userID)`, `SetUserCanInvite`.
- The typeahead of step 2 (`user-typeahead.html`, a suggestions endpoint)
  is gone. Its notes, for another time: jq79 writes `hidden="false"` for a
  false binding (bind `null`), and `.form-row > [data-c79-box] > *` is
  `flex: none` on wide screens.

### Then: Shared with you (2026-11-26, after #30 was squashed into main)

"En el panel, debería salir los subdominios a los que un usuario ha sido
invitado".

- `GET /api/shared` (not under `/api/subdomains/`, where a subdomain named
  "shared" would clash): the subdomains the user was invited to that let
  them in now (`manage.SharedWith` over `store.InvitationsFor`): the owner
  is enabled and may sign in, and, while it is private, may invite. One
  that was made public or for signed-in users still shows.
- `api.Shared`: name, URL, owner's email and name, access, online and when
  they were invited. Nothing an owner sees about keys, ports or the
  tunnel's client.
- The dashboard's home loads it with the user's subdomains (prefetched
  too) and shows a "Shared with you" card under them when there are any:
  host (opens the tunnel), status, owner, when, an Open button; no Manage.

## Privacy

Users who may invite see the emails and names of the accounts they can
invite. That is why it is a right an administrator gives, not everyone's.
The sign-in form still reveals nothing to someone not signed in.
